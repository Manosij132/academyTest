Evaluation Instructions

videocam Please Read Carefully Before Proceeding

This is a video-based assessment evaluation. Your responses will be recorded and may be shared with relevant account teams for evaluation against their open positions.

Before You Begin

  • You are in a quiet, distraction-free environment with minimal background noise.
  • Your surroundings are well-lit and your face is clearly visible on camera.
  • Your camera and microphone are functioning properly.
  • You have a stable internet connection to avoid interruptions.
  • Do not refresh, close, or navigate away from the browser during the evaluation, as this may terminate your session.

During the Evaluation

  • Ensure your camera remains switched on throughout the evaluation and is not paused or turned off at any time.
  • Speak clearly, confidently, and at a steady pace.
  • Provide detailed, structured, and well-explained answers, including:
    • Your thought process and approach
    • Relevant real-world examples
    • References to past project experience

Introduction Expectations

At the beginning of the evaluation, Globant AI will ask you to introduce yourself.

This is the first question, and it sets the context for the rest of the evaluation.

  • Please ensure your introduction clearly covers the following points:
    • Your total years of professional experience
    • Your primary technology stacks
    • Your areas of technical expertise
    • The domains/industries you have worked in
    • A brief mention of notable or impactful projects you have been involved in
  • Your introduction should be concise, well-structured, and informative, helping the evaluator quickly understand your background, skill set, and professional focus.

Pausing the Evaluation

  • You may pause the evaluation if required.
  • Important: Clicking the Pause button will skip the current question.
  • When you resume, the next question will appear automatically.
  • If you submit an answer before pausing, it will be recorded and counted as answered.

Evaluation and Next Steps

  • Your evaluation will be assessed based on:
    • Technical knowledge and problem-solving ability
    • Clarity and effectiveness of communication
    • Depth of explanation and real-world relevance
    • Overall professionalism and presentation
  • Candidates who receive higher ratings are more likely to be considered fit for relevant roles by participating accounts.
